-  Highest Conversion Device: **Desktop (10.58%)**

---

# Business Recommendations

### 1. Improve Checkout Process

A significant number of users drop between **Add to Cart** and **Checkout**. Simplifying the checkout process may improve conversions.

### 2. Increase Google Ads Investment

Google Ads generated the highest revenue and should be prioritized for future marketing campaigns.

### 3. Optimize Mobile Experience

Desktop users have the highest conversion rate. Improving the mobile experience may increase overall conversions.
